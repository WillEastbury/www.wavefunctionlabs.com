Plain English: The Transformer Model (LLMs)

A systems-level walkthrough of transformer inference for senior engineers. The running example for the entire book is the prompt "What is the capital of France?" and the goal is simple: explain, in precise engineering terms, how a static pile of matrices turns that prompt into the next token " Paris" and then keeps going.

dark theme · JetBrains Mono standalone chapter pages 15 chapters + glossary ⁇ wtf? · ψ deeper · 🦆 ducky toggles tabbed variants

What this book is trying to do: remove the fog. A transformer is not a bag of vibes. It is an execution engine with a tokenizer on the front, a sampler on the back, and a lot of linear algebra in the middle. Each chapter isolates one subsystem so you can see where the cost, state, and behavior actually live.

How to use this page

Audience

You already understand caches, pipelines, memory bandwidth, vectorized compute, and why data movement dominates more software than people admit.

Method

No hype, no mysticism, and no unexplained equations. Every symbolic step is immediately translated back into plain English.
